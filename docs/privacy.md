# MandalaThink 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective date: 2026-11-16

## 简体中文

MandalaThink 是一个九宫格笔记工具，无需创建 MandalaThink 账号。

- **项目内容。** 正文、颜色、行动及附件保存在项目文件中。项目可以存放在设备上，也可以使用 Apple 的 iCloud 云盘同步。MandalaThink 不运营接收项目文件的服务器。
- **设备权限。** 当你选择拍摄附件时，App 会请求相机权限。App 只导入你在系统照片选择器中明确选择的内容。当你选择将行动添加到 Apple 提醒事项时，App 会请求提醒事项权限。
- **分享和外部链接。** 导出或分享项目时，由你选择接收方。你打开的外部网站依其自身的隐私政策处理访问。
- **数据管理。** 你可以在 App 或系统文件管理器中管理项目，并在系统设置中调整相机、提醒事项和 iCloud 权限。已导出的副本和已创建的提醒事项可在各自的保存位置管理。
- **联系。** 隐私和使用问题可通过 [MandalaThink 支持页面](https://github.com/longbai/mandala-think/issues)反馈。该页面公开可见，请勿在其中发布私人项目或个人信息。

## English

MandalaThink is a grid-based note-taking tool. You can use it without creating a MandalaThink account.

- **Project content.** Notes, colors, actions, and attachments are stored in project files. You can keep projects on your device or sync through Apple’s iCloud Drive. MandalaThink does not operate a server that receives project files.
- **Device permissions.** The app asks for camera access when you choose to capture an attachment. It imports only items you select in the system photo picker. It requests Reminders access when you choose to add an action to Apple Reminders.
- **Sharing and links.** When you export or share a project, you choose the recipients. External sites you open apply their own privacy policies.
- **Data controls.** You can manage projects in MandalaThink or your system file manager, and adjust camera, Reminders, and iCloud permissions in system settings. Exported copies and created reminders can be managed where they were saved.
- **Contact.** Contact the developer about privacy or app support through the [MandalaThink support page](https://github.com/longbai/mandala-think/issues). This page is public. Keep personal information and private projects out of your post.
